CMCHIS enrolment income limit - Tamil Nadu Government Order G.O. (Ms) No. 560
(English transcription of the Tamil original, prepared for this corpus. The source document is a two-page scanned PDF in Tamil published on the official CMCHIS portal; it has no text layer, so this is a hand transcription and translation, not OCR output.)

Issuing department: Health and Family Welfare (EAP-1) Department, Government of Tamil Nadu
Order: G.O. (Ms) No. 560, dated 16.12.2021
Subject: Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme - raising the annual family income limit for becoming a beneficiary of the scheme from Rs. 72,000 to Rs. 1,20,000.

Background stated in the order
- The Kalaignar Insurance Scheme was started by the Tamil Nadu Government on 23.07.2009 so that poor and low-income people could get life-saving medical treatment free of charge in government and private hospitals. Each beneficiary family was insured up to Rs. 1.00 lakh.
- As a continuation, the Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme has been implemented in expanded form from 11.01.2012. Under G.O. (Ms) No. 169 of the Health and Family Welfare (EAP 2-(2)) Department dated 11.07.2011, families with an annual family income below Rs. 72,000 were included in the scheme, and the annual income limit has remained unchanged until now.
- The scheme has been integrated with the Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) and the insurance cover for all beneficiaries of the Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme has been raised to Rs. 5.00 lakh.
- Based on requests and opinions received from various quarters, the Director of the Tamil Nadu Health Systems Project asked in his letter that the annual family income limit currently fixed at Rs. 72,000 be raised to Rs. 1,20,000.

Order
- After careful consideration of the Director's request, the Government orders that, for the scheme to be extended afresh from 11.01.2022, the annual family income limit for becoming a beneficiary of the Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme is raised from Rs. 72,000 to Rs. 1,20,000.
- The order is issued with the concurrence of the Finance Department.

Signed by the Principal Secretary to Government, 16.12.2021. Received at the Tamil Nadu Health Systems Project, DMS Campus, Chennai on 24 December 2021.

Source note: the same portal lists this order as "CMCHIS Scheme Enrollment Criteria - Annual Income revised to Rs. 1,20,000/-" (G.O No 560) and its eligibility page quotes Rs. 1,20,000 per year citing G.O. (Ms) No. 560, dated 16.12.2021. One page on the same portal (the enrollment checker) still shows the older Rs. 72,000 figure, which this order supersedes.
