69. CHIEF MINISTER’S COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH INSURANCE SCHEME

                                 Government has implemented a comprehensive health

                                 persons. Under this scheme, all the differently abled
                                 persons and their family members are enrolled without
                                 any income ceiling.
