Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

The Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) is a flagship initiative by the Tamil Nadu Government aimed at increasing healthcare access and security for economically vulnerable families across the state. Initially launched as Kalaignar Kaappittu Thittam in July 2009, CMCHIS is currently operated by United India Insurance Company and covers around 1.48 crore families as of 2024, continuing through 2027.

Enrollment: the portal provides enrollment-related services to manage a family's healthcare benefits.
